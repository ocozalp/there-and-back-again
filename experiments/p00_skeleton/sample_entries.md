# Sample captions and diary entries

- **Bilbo in the mountains, at night, in the rain**  
  Rain again under the stars, high in the mountains. I miss my armchair.
- **Bilbo in a cave, at midday, in the sun**  
  The sun was out in the afternoon, underground in the dark. Not a bad day for a hobbit.
- **Bilbo in the Shire, at dawn, in the snow, with an eagle overhead**  
  Cold, and snowing as the sun came up, at home in the Shire. An eagle circled far overhead. Walked a long way.
- **Bilbo in the Shire, at dawn, in the rain, with an eagle overhead, holding a ring**  
  It rained without pause as the sun came up, at home in the Shire. A great eagle watched us from the sky. The ring felt heavier today. Slept badly, dreamt of eggs.
- **Bilbo in a cave, at midday, in the fog, with a dragon in the distance**  
  Fog so thick I could not see my own feet through the day, underground in the dark. Far off, something large and red moved across the sky. I did not sleep well. I wish I had never left Bag End.
- **Bilbo in the Shire, at dawn, in the sun, with an eagle overhead, holding a ring**  
  Sunshine as the sun came up, among the green hills of the Shire. A great eagle watched us from the sky. I turned the ring over in my pocket and told no one. I thought fondly of second breakfast.
- **Bilbo by the river, at dusk, in the snow, with a dwarf, holding a ring**  
  Snow fell as the light faded, on the riverbank. One of the dwarves kept me company and complained about the food. The ring felt heavier today. Walked a long way.
- **Bilbo in a cave, at night, in the fog, with a dwarf, by a campfire**  
  A grey fog hung over everything by night, underground in the dark. One of the dwarves kept me company and complained about the food. A small campfire, and stories until late. Not a bad day for a hobbit.
